# Credentials

This page separates what an individual can earn independently from what requires partner access or employer nomination. Claims are taken only from the primary Anthropic sources cited, with access dates.

## Available independently

### Claude Academy completion badges

- Listed on many, not all, public Academy course cards. See [official-catalog.md](official-catalog.md) for each course.
- These are records of course completion. They are **not** a professional certification.
- Source: <https://academy.claude.com/courses> (accessed 2026-10-24)

## Not generally available independently

### Anthropic certification (Partner Academy)

- Anthropic states: "Certifications belong to individual people, not firms, and are earned through Anthropic Partner Academy exams."
- Firms in the Claude Partner Network's Services Track "gain Anthropic Partner Academy access, including certification exams."
- **Uncertain:** the source does not say whether individuals outside a partner firm can take the exams.
- Source: <https://www.anthropic.com/news/services-track-partner-hub> (published 2026-06-03, accessed 2026-10-24)

### Claude Frontier Academy badges

- **Claude Resident Engineer badge:** earned by engineers who pass the graded practical at the end of the initial in-person program. They then move into the 12-week residency.
- **Claude Frontier Deployed Engineer badge:** earned by passing the reassessment at the end of the 12-week residency. Anthropic expects the first to be awarded in early 2027.
- **Eligibility:** participation is by nomination, and organizations nominate their engineers. Anthropic describes candidates as "hands-on software engineers with strong fundamentals, a record of building with LLMs," with "experience helping others adopt AI." It also states that prior experience building AI agents is not required. Each nominee arrives with a named Claude project to lead.
- Source: <https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-frontier-academy> (published 2026-10-02, accessed 2026-10-24)
